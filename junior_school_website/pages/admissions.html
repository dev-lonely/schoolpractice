ADMISSIONS

Start your Greenfield journey.

We welcome families who are looking for a supportive environment where students can learn, develop and feel part of a community.

How to Apply

Our admissions team can guide you through the process and explain the requirements for your child's intended class.

  1. Make an enquiry

    Contact the school office to ask about available places, fees and entry requirements.

  2. Complete an application

    Collect and submit the application form with the requested student information.

  3. Attend an assessment or meeting

    Where required, the school will arrange an age-appropriate assessment or family meeting.

  4. Receive a decision

    The school will explain the next steps and provide enrolment information to successful applicants.

Application Checklist

Families may be asked to provide the following documents. Confirm the current requirements with the school office.

  • Completed application form
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Previous school reports, if applicable
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Parent or guardian contact details

Need help?

Contact our school office for guidance on the application process.
