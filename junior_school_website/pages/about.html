GET TO KNOW US

A school built around every learner.

We believe that a good education helps students become thoughtful, capable and caring members of their community.

Our Story

Greenfield Academy was established to provide a welcoming place where children can learn, explore their interests and prepare for future opportunities. Our school community brings students, teachers and families together around a shared commitment to learning.

We value steady progress, honest effort and the understanding that every learner has strengths worth developing.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission

To provide inclusive, high-quality education that develops knowledge, confidence, responsibility and creativity.

Our vision

To be a trusted learning community that prepares young people to contribute positively to society.

Our Values

  • Respect: We listen to others and treat people fairly.
  • Integrity: We act honestly and take responsibility.
  • Curiosity: We ask questions and keep learning.
  • Teamwork: We support one another and work together.

Our Approach

Our approach balances classroom learning with practical activities, collaboration and opportunities to take responsibility. Teachers guide students to think independently while providing support when they need it.
